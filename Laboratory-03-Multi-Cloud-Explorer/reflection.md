# Mission Reflection

## Which cloud platform impressed me the most?
Among the three cloud platforms, Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most because of its leadership in Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes. Google created Kubernetes, and its managed service, GKE, is considered the most mature in the industry. I was also amazed by how GCP leverages the same infrastructure that powers Google Search, Gmail, and YouTube, giving it a strong foundation for high-performance computing and data analytics.

## What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
All three cloud providers share several core similarities. They offer compute, storage, networking, and identity management services, just with different names—like EC2, Virtual Machines, and Compute Engine. They all provide global infrastructure through regions and availability zones, pay-as-you-go pricing models, and free tiers for beginners. They also emphasize security, scalability, and reliability, making them all viable options for enterprise workloads.

## How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?
Business requirements heavily influence cloud platform selection because no single cloud is best for every scenario. A startup with a limited budget may prefer AWS for its flexible pricing, while a university already using Microsoft products would benefit most from Azure's native integration. An AI research company would lean toward GCP for its TPUs and Vertex AI, while a global e-commerce company would choose AWS for its extensive global reach and auto-scaling capabilities. The right choice depends on cost, existing technology, workload type, and scalability needs.

## What did I learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?
Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to think beyond technical features and focus on the client's actual business problems. I learned to analyze scenarios, weigh trade-offs, and justify recommendations with evidence. I also realized that being a consultant means being objective—choosing the platform that best solves the problem, not just the most popular one.
